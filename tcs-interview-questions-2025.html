TCS Interview Questions 2025 — HR & Technical Round Answers

Updated: June 2026 • 8 min read

Tata Consultancy Services (TCS) is one of the biggest IT recruiters in India. Cracking the TCS interview process (Ninja or Digital) in 2025 requires more than just technical knowledge—it requires passing the tricky HR and Managerial rounds where many candidates fail.

TCS Hiring Process 2025 Overview

The standard TCS recruitment process consists of 3 stages:

  1. TCS NQT (National Qualifier Test): The aptitude and coding written test.
  2. Technical Round (TR): Core subjects (C, C++, Java, Python, SQL), your resume, and final year projects.
  3. HR & Managerial Round (MR/HR): Culture fit, adaptability, willingness to relocate, and communication skills.

Top 10 TCS HR Round Questions (With Sample Answers)

TCS HR rounds test your reliability. They are looking for long-term employees, not job-hoppers.

1. Are you willing to relocate to any location in India?
"Yes, absolutely. I am quite flexible and eager to start my career with TCS. Relocating would give me a great opportunity to explore a new city, meet different people, and learn to be more independent, which I see as a positive step for my personal and professional growth."
Pro Tip: Always say YES. TCS has offices across India and requires flexibility. Saying no here is an immediate red flag.
2. Are you comfortable working in night shifts?
"Yes, I am comfortable with rotational and night shifts. I understand that TCS serves global clients across different time zones, and adapting to their business hours is part of the role."
3. What do you know about TCS?
"TCS is a global leader in IT services, consulting, and business solutions, part of the Tata Group. It was founded in 1968. The current CEO is K. Krithivasan. TCS operates in over 50 countries and is known for its employee-friendly policies and strong ethics."
Pro Tip: Mention the CEO's name and read up on any recent TCS news or acquisitions. It shows you've done your homework.
4. Why should we hire you?
"As a fresher, I bring a strong foundation in [your skills, e.g., Java and SQL] and a quick learning attitude. During my final year project on [Project Name], I learned how to work in a team and meet deadlines. I am adaptable and eager to contribute to TCS's growth while building a long-term career here."

Top 8 TCS Technical Round Questions

For freshers, TCS focuses heavily on fundamentals rather than complex system design.

  • OOPs Concepts: Explain Polymorphism, Inheritance, Encapsulation, and Abstraction with real-world examples.
  • SQL Basics: Differences between TRUNCATE, DELETE, and DROP. Different types of JOINs.
  • Data Structures: Array vs LinkedList. Stack vs Queue.
  • Your Resume Projects: Be prepared to draw the architecture of your final year project and explain the role you played.
  • Coding: Write a program to reverse a string, check for a palindrome, or find the factorial of a number without using inbuilt functions.

TCS Salary Packages for Freshers 2025

  • TCS Ninja: ~3.36 LPA (Base package for IT roles)
  • TCS Digital: ~7.0 LPA (Requires clearing advanced coding section in NQT)
  • TCS Prime: ~9.0 LPA (For top-tier talent, specialized roles)

Tips to Crack the Final HR Round

  1. Dress Code: Strictly formal. Tie is recommended for men. Neat grooming is non-negotiable.
  2. Body Language: Sit straight, maintain eye contact, and smile. TCS values politeness and respect.
  3. Be Honest: If you don't know a technical answer, just say "I am not sure about this right now, but I will definitely read up on it." Do not bluff.

FAQ: TCS Joining and Offers

How long does it take to get the TCS offer letter?

After the HR round, it generally takes 2 to 4 weeks for the status to change to "Offered" on the NextStep portal.

What happens in the background check?

TCS will verify your 10th, 12th, and degree marksheets, address proof, and any gaps in education. Make sure everything you mentioned on your resume is 100% true.
