Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

CareerAI India ("we", "our", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our website and AI-powered career tools.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: Your name, email address, and password when you sign up.
  • Google Sign-In data: Your name, email, and profile photo if you sign in with Google.
  • Content you enter: Resume details, job experience, skills, and other career information you provide to our AI tools.
  • Payment information: Your UPI Transaction Reference (UTR) number for verifying payments. We do not store your bank account, UPI PIN, or any sensitive financial credentials.
  • Usage data: How many times you use each tool, which tools you use, and basic browser/device information.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To generate AI-powered resumes, cover letters, interview feedback, and other career content using Google Gemini AI.
  • To verify your UPI payment and activate your subscription.
  • To save your resumes and preferences to your account dashboard.
  • To send you important updates about your account via WhatsApp or email (only when necessary).
  • To improve our AI tools and user experience.

3. AI & Data Processing

Your career data (name, experience, skills) is sent to Google Gemini AI API to generate AI responses. Google processes this data according to their Gemini API Terms and privacy policy. We do not sell your data to any third party.

4. Firebase & Data Storage

We use Google Firebase for authentication and database storage. Your account data is stored securely in Firebase Firestore. Firebase is compliant with GDPR, ISO 27001, and SOC 2/3 security standards.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your language preference, free trial usage count, and session state. No third-party tracking cookies are used.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account data as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us at support@careerai.in.

7. Children's Privacy

CareerAI India is intended for users aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 16.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We'll notify you of significant changes via email or an in-app notice.
