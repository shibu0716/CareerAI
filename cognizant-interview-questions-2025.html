Cognizant Hiring Process 2025 Overview

The standard Cognizant recruitment process consists of 3 stages:

  1. Written Test: Aptitude, logical reasoning, and coding.
  2. Technical Round (TR): Core subjects, resume deep-dive, and projects.
  3. HR & Managerial Round (MR/HR): Culture fit, adaptability, and communication skills.

Top 10 Cognizant HR Round Questions

Cognizant HR rounds test your reliability. They are looking for long-term employees, not job-hoppers.

1. Are you willing to relocate to any location in India?
"Yes, absolutely. I am quite flexible and eager to start my career with Cognizant. Relocating would give me a great opportunity to explore a new city, meet different people, and learn to be more independent."
Pro Tip: Always say YES. Large IT companies have offices across India and require flexibility.
2. Are you comfortable working in night shifts?
"Yes, I am comfortable with rotational and night shifts. I understand that Cognizant serves global clients across different time zones, and adapting to their business hours is part of the role."
3. Why should we hire you?
"As a fresher, I bring a strong foundation in problem-solving and a quick learning attitude. During my final year project, I learned how to work in a team and meet deadlines. I am eager to contribute to Cognizant's growth while building a long-term career here."
