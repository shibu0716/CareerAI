UPSC Personality Test 2025 — Mock Interviews & DAF Tips

Updated: June 2026 • 7 min read

The UPSC Personality Test (Interview) carries 275 marks. Often, it's the difference between becoming an IAS officer, getting a lower service, or missing the list entirely. Unlike the Mains, this is not a test of your knowledge—it is a test of your character, logic, and temperament under pressure.

What the UPSC Board Looks For

The board members are experienced administrators and academics. They are evaluating you on:

  • Mental alertness and critical powers of assimilation
  • Clear and logical exposition
  • Balance of judgement
  • Variety and depth of interest
  • Ability for social cohesion and leadership
  • Intellectual and moral integrity

Top 5 Most Common UPSC Interview Questions

1. Tell us briefly about yourself.
This is usually the ice-breaker. Do not recite your DAF. Keep it concise (1-2 minutes). Highlight your educational background, your home state/district, and perhaps one key hobby or passion that defines your personality.
2. Why do you want to join the Civil Services?
Avoid cliché answers like "I want to serve the nation." Everyone says that. Base your answer on a personal experience, a realization during your college days/previous job, or a specific problem you want to solve at a large scale. Be authentic.
3. (DAF Based) Why did you choose [Optional Subject] when your graduation was in [Different Subject]?
A very common question for engineers who take Arts optionals. Be honest. Was it interest? Was it scoring? Did you read a book that inspired you? A logical, honest answer is always better than a made-up philosophical one.
4. What is the biggest problem in your home state/district, and how would you solve it?
You are expected to know your home district thoroughly—its geography, economy, social issues, and politics. Do not just state the problem; offer a pragmatic, administrative solution.
5. Situational: You are the DM/SP of a district where a communal riot is about to break out. What are your immediate steps?
Testing your crisis management. Focus on logical steps: 1. Information gathering & verification. 2. Preventive arrests/Section 144. 3. Deployment of forces. 4. Engaging community leaders (Peace Committee). 5. Controlling social media rumors.

How to Prepare Your DAF

Your Detailed Application Form (DAF) is the syllabus for your interview. Prepare notes on:

  • The meaning of your name, your parents' names.
  • Every city/town you have lived in or studied in.
  • Your graduation subject—know the current applications of your degree.
  • Your hobbies—prepare in depth. If you wrote "reading," know the authors, genres, and recent books you've read.

Dress Code & Body Language

Men: A well-fitted suit or a formal shirt with trousers and a tie. Ensure you are cleanly shaven or have a neatly trimmed beard.
Women: A formal saree, salwar kameez, or formal western wear. Keep accessories minimal.

Body Language: Walk confidently. Wish the Chairperson first, then the lady members (if any), then the rest. Wait to be asked to sit. Maintain calm eye contact with the person asking the question, but glance at others while answering.
