Common Questions
How do I get my referral link?

After you apply, we review your application and WhatsApp you a unique link like careerai.in/?ref=YOURCODE within 24 hours. Share this link anywhere and every sale is tracked to you.

When do I get paid?

Payouts happen on the 5th of every month directly to your UPI ID. There is no minimum threshold — even 1 referral gets paid.

How are referrals tracked?

Your referral link adds a cookie (30-day duration) to the visitor's browser. If they subscribe within 30 days of clicking your link, you get credit — even if they come back later.

Is there a limit on how much I can earn?

No limit at all. The more people you refer, the more you earn. Some of our top partners are on track to earn ₹5,000–₹15,000/month.

Can I join if I'm a student with no audience?

Yes! Even sharing with 20–30 classmates in a WhatsApp group can get you 5–10 subscribers a month, earning ₹150–₹300 extra. Many of our affiliates started as students.
